# Visual guidelines

## Style

- Modern business-tech editorial illustration
- Flat vector or soft 3D, not photorealistic unless explicitly requested
- White space, clear silhouette, one focal point
- Navy `#001B4D`, green `#00A83B`, blue `#1D74E8`, cyan `#17B8C8`
- Avoid purple, neon, noisy backgrounds, tiny decorative details

## Output

- Prefer PNG with transparency for conceptual illustrations.
- Prefer SVG/CSS for exact diagrams and charts.
- Generate at least 2x the CSS display size.
- Keep 10 percent safe padding on every edge.
- Do not include text, letters, numbers, UI labels, brand marks, or watermarks.

## Review

Inspect every generated file. Reject malformed hands or faces when people are present, accidental text, clipped arrows, muddy contrast, and compositions that require `object-fit: cover`.
